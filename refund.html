1. Statutory Legal Framework & Right of Withdrawal Waiver

Under consumer protection statutes governing online commercial transactions, consumers are ordinarily granted a statutory 14-day withdrawal period for distant contracts. However, applicable laws explicitly provide a statutory exception for the immediate supply of digital content and services:

  • European Union Directive 2011/83/EU (Consumer Rights Directive), Article 16(m): The statutory right of withdrawal does not apply to contracts for the supply of digital content which is not supplied on a tangible medium if the performance has begun with the consumer's prior express consent and their acknowledgment that they thereby lose their right of withdrawal.
  • United Kingdom Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, Regulation 37: Statutory cooling-off rights cease as soon as digital content downloads or streaming/server provisioning commences with express customer agreement.
  • Republic of Serbia Law on Consumer Protection (Zakon o zaštiti potrošača, Član 37. stav 1. tačka 13): Isključenje prava na odustanak od ugovora zaključenog na daljinu za isporuku digitalnog sadržaja koji nije isporučen na trajnom nosaču zapisa, ako je izvršenje započelo posle prethodnog izričitog pristanka potrošača i njegove potvrde da zna da time gubi pravo na odustanak od ugovora.
  • United States Federal Trade Commission (FTC) & Digital Commerce Acts: All digital transactions with immediate fulfillment are recognized as final sales once the digital entitlement is delivered to the user account.

By clicking Unlock GEO Ride Pro and completing checkout, you provide your express prior request and authorization for immediate execution of the contract and explicitly acknowledge that your right of withdrawal is waived in full.
