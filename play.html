GLOBAL DESTINATIONS

1-Click Teleport to the most legendary roads, passes, and circuits on Earth

JAPAN • STREET DRIFT • CITY
Tokyo Shuto Expressway (Tokyo Drift)

World capital of midnight street racing, Wangan battles, and iconic Tokyo Drift culture. Multi-tier elevated highway system slicing through illuminated Shinjuku skyscrapers above Tokyo Bay.

MONACO • FORMULA 1 CIRCUIT
Monaco GP Marina Circuit

The crown jewel of Formula 1 racing since 1929. World-famous for surgical precision past superyachts, the legendary Casino Square hairpin, and the echoing coastal tunnel roar.

GERMANY • MOTORSPORT CIRCUIT
Nürburgring Nordschleife (The Green Hell)

The Green Hell: the universal proving ground for automotive performance. 20.8 km of unforgiving tarmac, severe elevation plunges, and over 150 corners carved into the Eifel mountains.

UK • FORMULA 1 CIRCUIT
Silverstone Grand Prix Circuit

The historic home of British motorsport and high-speed aerodynamics. Full throttle sweeps through Maggotts, Becketts, Chapel, and the celebrated Stowe corner.

BELGIUM • FORMULA 1 CIRCUIT
Circuit de Spa-Francorchamps (Eau Rouge)

Rollercoaster motorsport Mecca hidden in the Ardennes forest. Legendary for the heart-stopping compression of Eau Rouge, the climb of Raidillon, and ultra-fast Kemmel Straight.

USA • MOTORSPORT CIRCUIT
Laguna Seca Raceway (The Corkscrew)

Monterey's world-famous road course crowned by Turn 8 and 8A: The Corkscrew, a blinding 5.5-story drop where cars plummet down 59 vertical feet through left-right camber sweeps.

ITALY • ALPINE PASS
Stelvio Pass (Passo dello Stelvio)

Hailed as the greatest driving road on Earth. 48 numbered hairpin switchbacks ascending to 2,757 meters altitude with dramatic Alpine mountain drop-offs.

ROMANIA • CARPATHIAN PASS
Transfăgărășan Mountain Highway

Carpathian mountain masterpiece built across Romania's highest peaks. Miles of unbroken S-curves, viaduct bridges, and glacial valleys celebrated across global car culture.

USA • SMOKY MOUNTAINS • PASS
Tail of the Dragon (318 Mountain Curves)

America's top curve-carving road: 318 relentless curves packed into just 11 miles bordering the Great Smoky Mountains National Park with zero intersecting driveways.

USA • STEEP SWITCHBACKS • PASS
San Francisco Lombard Street Hairpins

The world's most crooked street. Eight sharp cobblestone switchbacks snaking down a 27% downhill grade on Russian Hill, immortalized in countless cinematic car chases.

USA • CANYON RIDGE • PASS
Mulholland Drive Canyon Ridge

Legendary canyon ridgeline towering above the Hollywood Hills and Los Angeles basin. The historic playground of nocturnal canyon carvers and sports car culture.

FRANCE • HISTORIC ROUNDABOUT • CITY
Arc de Triomphe & Champs-Élysées

The most thrilling multi-lane roundabout on Earth. Twelve massive Parisian avenues converge around Napoleon's monument with zero lane markings, demanding total driver reflexes.

UK • ROYAL EMBANKMENT • CITY
Westminster & Big Ben Thames Route

Classic British motoring along the River Thames embankment. Cross iconic Westminster Bridge with Big Ben and Parliament looming overhead through tight London corridors.

UAE • HYPERCAR HIGHWAY • CITY
Dubai Sheikh Zayed 14-Lane Skyline

Futuristic 14-lane hypercar freeway cutting straight through the world's most spectacular skyscraper corridor under dazzling desert skyline lighting.

USA • OCEAN CRUISE • CITY
Miami Ocean Drive & South Beach

Iconic palm-lined coastal avenue facing South Beach. Past pastel Art Deco hotels, neon facades, and cruising supercars under the vibrant Atlantic Florida sun.

GERMANY • HISTORIC SPEEDWAY • CITY
Berlin Autobahn & Tiergarten

Grand German boulevard sweeps passing the Brandenburg Gate, victory column, and leading directly to the legendary speed-unrestricted Autobahn network.

SERBIA • BASE HQ • CITY
Belgrade Waterfront (GEO Ride HQ)

The official spawn ground and birthplace of GEO Ride. Wide riverside boulevards, glass tower architecture, and expansive asphalt sweeps directly along the Sava river.
