OFFICIAL GAME OVERVIEW & SIMULATOR MANUAL

GEO Ride: 3D Real-World Browser Driving Simulator

GEO Ride is a free browser-based 3D real-world driving simulator created by Milan Petkovski (Milan Web Portal) that lets you drive realistic vehicles on any road, street address, or racing circuit on planet Earth with zero downloads. Powered by Mapbox GL JS v3.3.0 satellite and street vector cartography combined with a custom Three.js r147 WebGL 2.0 Newtonian physics engine, it delivers 60 FPS driving, handbrake drifting, extruded 3D buildings, and instant global multiplayer directly in your desktop or mobile browser.

CONTROLS, VEHICLES & PHYSICS DEFINITIONS
Desktop Keyboard Controls
Press W / A / S / D or Arrow Keys to accelerate, brake, reverse, and steer. Hold Spacebar to initiate a handbrake drift and build combo multipliers (x1 to x10). Press R for local road reset, Shift + R for global spawn reset, C to cycle camera angles, and M to toggle Web Radio.
Mobile & Tablet Touch Controls
Use the dedicated multi-touch on-screen GAS, BRAKE, DRIFT, and RESET pedals with left/right steering buttons, or enable gyroscope Tilt Steering in Settings > Driving with Low, Normal, or High sensitivity.
Vehicle Physics Profiles (Car, Truck, Bus, God Mode)
Sports Car features fast acceleration, high top speed, and responsive drift slip (slipGrip: 0.05). Heavy Truck delivers high-mass momentum and wider cornering arcs. City Bus simulates long-wheelbase urban transit handling. God Mode (Pro) removes building collision clipping and unlocks extreme velocity.
Ackermann Steering & Lateral Slip Grip
A client-side Newtonian physics model calculating longitudinal traction, centrifugal body roll, dynamic tire skidmarks, and lateral slip angles at 60 Hz, allowing controlled counter-steer drifting across real-world road geometry.
3D Building Extrusion Raycasting
Real-time spatial collision detection against Mapbox vector building footprints, triggering directional impact rebound and camera shake when colliding with urban structures.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)

What is GEO Ride and how does this 3D browser driving simulator work?

GEO Ride is a free browser-based 3D real-world driving simulator created by Milan Petkovski. It combines Mapbox GL JS v3.3.0 satellite and street cartography with a custom Three.js r147 WebGL 2.0 physics engine, allowing players to drive realistic 3D vehicles anywhere on Earth without downloading any software.

Can I drive on my own street or hometown in a web browser?

Yes, you can drive on your own street or any hometown worldwide directly in your browser. Use the built-in location search bar in the top header or inside Settings > General to enter any city, street address, or landmark, and GEO Ride will instantly teleport your vehicle to that exact coordinate.

Is GEO Ride a free 3D driving game without download or installation?

Yes, GEO Ride is 100% free to play in any modern web browser with zero downloads, plugins, or registration required. All core features, including 3 vehicles (Car, Truck, Bus), global location search, 17 curated destinations, 4 map modes, and public multiplayer lobbies, are permanently free.

What vehicles can I drive in GEO Ride and how do their physics differ?

GEO Ride features 4 selectable vehicles in Settings > Driving: Sports Car (agile acceleration, tight turning, and responsive drift slip), Heavy Truck (high mass momentum and wider turning radius), City Bus (long wheelbase urban transit handling), and Pro God Mode (a collision-free high-speed ghost vehicle).

Which 17 famous racing circuits, mountain passes, and cities are included?

Settings > Places includes 17 instant-teleport locations across 3 categories: 5 Racing Circuits (Monaco Grand Prix, Nürburgring Nordschleife, Silverstone, Spa-Francorchamps, Laguna Seca), 5 Mountain Passes (Stelvio Pass, Transfăgărășan, Tail of the Dragon, Mulholland Drive, San Francisco Lombard Street), and 7 Global Cities (Tokyo, Paris, London, Dubai, Miami, Berlin, Belgrade Waterfront).

How does real-time multiplayer work in GEO Ride without an account?

Multiplayer in GEO Ride requires no login or account. Open Settings > Multiplayer, enter a custom Pilot Callsign, and click Quick Join Global Lobby or enter a friend's room code. Vehicle coordinates, heading angles, and models synchronize in real time over WebSockets/MQTT (up to 5 players free, up to 10 in Pro).

What are the keyboard and mobile touch controls in GEO Ride?

On desktop keyboards, press W/A/S/D or Arrow keys to drive and steer, Spacebar to drift, R to reset your vehicle on the road, Shift+R for global spawn reset, C to cycle cameras, and M to toggle Web Radio. On mobile and tablets, use the on-screen GAS, BRAKE, DRIFT, and RESET pedals or tilt steering.

How do the drift physics and combo score multiplier work?

Holding Spacebar or the DRIFT pedal reduces rear lateral tire grip (slipGrip 0.05) and increases turn authority, letting you slide through corners while leaving dynamic tire skidmarks and smoke. Continuous drifting accumulates real-time points in the Drift HUD and builds a combo multiplier from x1 up to x10.

Can I play GEO Ride on mobile phones and tablets with tilt steering?

Yes, GEO Ride is fully responsive on iOS and Android phones and tablets. It provides dedicated multi-touch GAS, BRAKE, DRIFT, and RESET controls, optional gyroscope Tilt Steering with adjustable sensitivity (Low, Normal, High), haptic vibration feedback, and full-screen PWA home-screen installation.

What map styles, 3D building modes, and lighting presets are available?

In Settings > Map & 3D, you can switch between 4 map styles (Streets, Satellite, Hybrid, and Standard 3D WOW), toggle 3D Vehicles, Extruded 3D Buildings, and Street Labels, and select 5 atmospheric lighting presets (Real Time local sun position, Day, Dawn, Dusk, and Night).

What does GEO Ride Pro unlock and how does God Mode work?

GEO Ride Pro ($4.99/month or $2.99/month billed annually) unlocks the God Mode ghost vehicle (unlimited speed, extreme acceleration, and zero building collision clipping), private multiplayer rooms for up to 10 players, an exclusive Pro crown badge in multiplayer, and an ad-free driving experience.

What are the minimum browser and hardware requirements for 60 FPS?

GEO Ride runs in any modern browser supporting WebGL 2.0 (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari, Brave, Opera). It automatically profiles your GPU and offers 3 manual Graphics Quality tiers in Settings > Map & 3D: Ultra (native 2K/4K pixel ratio), Balanced (1080p), and Eco (1.0x pixel ratio for integrated GPUs and battery saving).
